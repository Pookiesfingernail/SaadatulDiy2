About Sa’adatulD.I.Y

SaadatulDIY is a handmade business specialising in unique, customised crochet creations. Each piece is carefully handcrafted with creativity and attention to detail.

Every crochet piece is made with the customer’s ideas in mind, with my own creative touch added to make each creation unique. I enjoy turning customers’ ideas into something special that they won’t find anywhere else.

I believe handmade gifts should be as unique as the people receiving them. My goal is to turn creative ideas into meaningful keepsakes that customers can treasure, display, and proudly call their own.

My Story

Nur Basyirah
Sa’adatul Hanan

I started SaadatulDIY in 2017, creating personalised yarn lettering and string art before expanding into customised brick designs and crochet.

With my background in architecture, I’ve always enjoyed designing and creating things. However, after developing carpal tunnel syndrome from managing my studies and workload, I began exploring crochet as a different creative outlet, making unique items that are hard to find in local businesses.

Now, I focus on creating customised crochet dolls, keychains, and other handmade pieces based on what my customers want. I enjoy bringing their ideas to life while adding my own personal touch to every creation.
